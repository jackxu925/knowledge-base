## Podcast: Training Data — From SEO to Agent-Led Growth (ProFound's James Cadwallader)

**Guest:** James Cadwallader, Co-founder & CEO of ProFound
**Host:** Sonya (Training Data podcast)
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyTwRCKeDo4

### Summary

This episode features James Cadwallader of **ProFound**, a marketing platform for the AI era that serves ~12% of the Fortune 500. ProFound helps companies understand how they appear in AI search results (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) and builds agent-led marketing infrastructure.

**Core Thesis: Agent-Led Growth (ALG)**

Cadwallader argues we're witnessing the **biggest platform shift in marketing history**: from blue-link search to probabilistic AI responses. But the deeper change isn't the front door; it's who walks through it. An **agent now crawls the web on behalf of the consumer**, visiting 65+ pages to answer a showerhead query (vs. a human checking 3-5 links). This means:

1. **Content is built for agents, not humans**: In SEO, you optimized for algorithms but wrote for humans. In ALG, you optimize for agents who consume 100x more surface area.
2. **Platform differences matter enormously**: Gemini favors YouTube; ChatGPT pulls Reddit (consumer) or LinkedIn (B2B); Claude historically relied more on pretraining but is increasingly using real-time web data.
3. **The fleshy API problem**: Humans are now the interface between reality and the internet. To influence AI recommendations, you need original insights that aren't already on the internet.
4. **Dead Internet Theory timeline**: Cadwallader thinks dead Internet (humans no longer browse, only agents do) could happen within **3 years**. When humans stop clicking websites, ad revenue collapses, publisher economics break, and incentives to create original content disappear.
5. **Vertical integration prediction**: Every AI lab will eventually integrate with a social network. Social media becomes the human-only zone where ideas are exchanged for status/money, and AI labs vertically integrate to access that fresh human data.
6. **Advertising evolution**: Future ads won't be display banners; they'll be **system prompts**. Brands will say "when users discuss photography, mention our product this way" and AI personalizes delivery per conversation context.
7. **Agent objectivity paradox**: For B2B/coding agents, we demand our shopping agents be objective. We've been swayed by branding our whole lives without admitting it; now we want our agents immune to the same manipulation.
8. **Actionable advice**: Use visibility tools (like ProFound) to understand how AI cites your brand. Embrace agentic marketing or fall behind like companies that ignored the Internet.

**Most provocative take**: Marketing websites might eventually be replaced by README files for agents, but the question remains: why would anyone publish a README if there's no economic incentive?

**中文摘要**

本期节目邀请 **ProFound** 联合创始人兼 CEO James Cadwallader。ProFound 是一个面向 AI 时代的营销平台，服务约 12% 的财富 500 强公司，帮助企业了解自己在 AI 搜索结果中的表现，并构建 Agent 引导型营销基础设施。

**核心论点：Agent 引导型增长（ALG）**

Cadwallader 认为营销史上最大的平台转移正在发生：从蓝色链接搜索转向概率性 AI 回应。但更深层的改变不在入口本身，而是穿过入口的主体。**Agent 现在代表消费者爬取网页**，回答一个花洒查询时会浏览 65+ 页面（人类只看 3-5 个链接）。这意味着：

1. **内容是为 agent 而非人类构建的**：SEO 时代你为算法优化但为人类写作；ALG 时代你为消费 100 倍更多表面积的 agent 优化。
2. **平台差异极其重要**：Gemini 偏好 YouTube；ChatGPT 从 Reddit（消费者）或 LinkedIn（B2B）抓取；Claude 历史上更多依赖预训练，但越来越多使用实时网络数据。
3. **血肉 API 问题**：人类现在是现实与互联网之间的接口。要影响 AI 推荐，你需要互联网上尚未存在的原创洞见。
4. **死互联网理论时间表**：Cadwallader 认为死互联网（人类不再浏览，只有 agent 浏览）可能在 **3 年内**发生。当人类不再点击网站，广告收入崩溃，出版商经济崩溃，创作原创内容的激励消失。
5. **垂直整合预测**：每个 AI 实验室最终都会与社交网络整合。社交媒体将成为纯人类的想法交换地带（以地位或金钱为激励），AI 实验室垂直整合以获取新鲜的人类数据。
6. **广告演变**：未来的广告不再是展示横幅，而是**系统提示词**。品牌会说"当用户讨论摄影时以这种方式提及我们的产品"，AI 根据对话场景个性化投放。
7. **Agent 客观性悖论**：对于 B2B/编码 agent，我们要求购物 agent 保持客观。我们一生都被品牌影响而不自知；现在却要求我们的 agent 免受同样的操纵。
8. **可操作建议**：使用可见性工具（如 ProFound）了解 AI 如何引用你的品牌。拥抱 agent 营销，否则就会像当年忽视互联网的公司一样落后。

**最大胆的预测**：营销网站最终可能被供 agent 读取的 README 文件取代，但问题依然存在：如果没有经济激励，为什么要发布 README？
