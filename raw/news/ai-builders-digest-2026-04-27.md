### No Priors — Scaling Global Organizations in the Age of AI with ServiceNow CEO Bill McDermott

**The Takeaway:** Enterprise platforms aren't being replaced by LLMs — they're becoming the "AI control tower" that orchestrates language models, hyperscalers, and systems of record into true agentic businesses. The moat isn't code; it's workflow context accumulated over decades.

ServiceNow CEO Bill McDermott sat down with Sarah Guo for a wide-ranging conversation on leadership, enterprise AI, and the future of platform companies. Here are the key insights:

**SaaSpocalypse is overblown.** McDermott ran the math: replacing a simple ServiceNow application with a custom LLM solution costs 10x more when you factor in GPU costs, token costs, and human capital redirected from value-creating work. Enterprise platforms have accumulated decades of workflow context, departmental integrations, and business process knowledge that language models simply cannot replicate. Moreover, people forgive human mistakes but never forgive software mistakes — and LLMs are non-deterministic by nature.

**AI thinks, workflow acts.** The critical distinction McDermott draws: language models give excellent advice in milliseconds, but they don't close cases. A compensation issue on "Aisle 7" requires data from HR, finance, legal, compliance, and risk — flowing through multiple departments and databases before resolution. That's what workflow platforms do. The white paper on "Blueprint for Agentic Business" was apparently transformative for CEOs who didn't understand where LLMs end and platforms begin.

**The platform moat is horizontal + vertical depth.** ServiceNow integrates ~800 systems of record, uses zero-copy data sharing (no data movement, no risk), and offers real-time business process modification through AI tools embedded in the platform. Major customers now go live in under 30 days. 90% of customer service cases are already managed by agents.

**What's at risk vs. safe.** McDermott believes departmental single-function vendors are vulnerable — especially those not on a CEO's priority list. Companies spanning multiple departments or serving as critical systems of record with mass customization are safe and will be part of the future. ServiceNow's position: 85 billion workflows in flight, 7 trillion transactions on platform.

**Leadership philosophy rooted in a delicatessen.** At 16, McDermott bought a deli for $7,000 (with $0 down) by convincing suppliers to give him inventory on consignment. The lesson: know your customer intimately, give them exactly what they want. That same philosophy drove his legendary Xerox interview where he told the hiring manager he guaranteed his dad would see his employee badge that night — and got hired on the spot despite breaking protocol. His core belief: never stop giving people shots (opportunities). Emerson Fullwood, who hired him, still attends his CEO meetings 25 years later.

**The next 5 years at ServiceNow:** Net new headcount will dramatically reduce as agents handle supporting functions (finance, HR, services). Investment shifts to humans doing what agents can't: innovation engineering, relationship building, trust creation. McDermott predicts 2.2 billion agents entering the workforce in the next few years — more agents than people.

**核心观点：** 企业平台不会被 LLM 替代——它们正在成为"AI 控制塔"，协调语言模型、超大规模云商和系统记录，打造真正的 agent 化企业。护城河不是代码，而是数十年积累的工作流上下文。

ServiceNow CEO Bill McDermott 与 Sarah Guo 进行了一次关于领导力、企业 AI 和平台公司未来的广泛对话。以下是关键洞察：

**SaaSpocalypse（SaaS 末日论）被夸大了。** McDermott 算了一笔账：用自定义 LLM 方案替代一个简单的 ServiceNow 应用，在考虑 GPU 成本、token 成本以及从价值创造工作中转移出来的人力资本后，成本要高出 10 倍。企业平台积累了数十年的工作流上下文、部门集成和业务流程知识，语言模型根本无法复制。而且，人们会原谅人的错误，但永远不会原谅软件的错误——LLM 本质上是不确定性的。

**AI 思考，工作流行动。** McDermott 强调的关键区别：语言模型能在毫秒内给出优秀建议，但它不会结案。第 7 过道的薪酬问题需要 HR、财务、法务、合规和风险等多个部门的数据——经过多个部门和数据库流转才能解决。这正是工作流平台做的事。《Agent 业务蓝图》白皮书显然对那些不明白 LLM 和平台边界在哪里的 CEO 产生了革命性影响。

**平台的护城河在于横向 + 纵向深度。** ServiceNow 集成了约 800 个系统记录源，使用零拷贝数据共享（不移动数据，无风险），并通过嵌入平台的 AI 工具提供实时的业务流程修改能力。大客户现在不到 30 天即可上线。90% 的客户服务案例已由 agent 管理。

**什么有风险 vs 什么是安全的。** McDermott 认为单一职能的部门级供应商是脆弱的——尤其是那些不在 CEO 优先事项列表上的公司。跨多个部门运营或作为经过大量定制的关键系统记录的公司是安全的，将是未来的一部分。ServiceNow 的现状：850 亿个工作流运行中，7 万亿次平台交易。

**领导力哲学源于一家熟食店。** 16 岁时，McDermott 用 0 首付、7000 美元（分期付款）买下了一家熟食店，靠说服供应商以寄售方式提供库存做到了。秘诀：深入了解你的客户，给他们确切想要的东西。同样的理念驱动了他在 Xerox 的传奇面试——他告诉招聘经理保证爸爸当晚能看到他的员工工牌——尽管打破了常规还是当场被录用。核心理念：永远不要停止给人机会。当年招聘他的 Emerson Fullwood，25 年后的今天仍然参加他的 CEO 会议。

**ServiceNow 未来 5 年：** 净新增人数将大幅减少，因为 agent 将处理支持职能（财务、HR、服务）。投资转向人做 agent 不能做的事：创新工程、关系建立、信任创造。McDermott 预测未来几年将有 22 亿个 agent 进入劳动力市场——agent 比人还多。
