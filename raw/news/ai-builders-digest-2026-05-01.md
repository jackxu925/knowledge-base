### AI & I by Every — How Stripe Is Building for an Agent-native World

Dan Shipper talks with Emily Glassberg Sands, Head of Data and AI at Stripe, about how the company is preparing for a world where AI agents become predominant actors on the Internet — not just humans buying and selling from each other, but agents buying and selling from humans and from each other.

The core frame: "The Internet has this new kind of actor on it. Over time, these agents will become the predominant actors on the Internet." As that happens, every layer of the stack — payments, billing, fraud detection, identity — needs to evolve. LLM traffic to Stripe Docs is up 10x year over year, which is a useful signal that machines are becoming users of developer infrastructure too.

Fraud has moved up the stack: it used to mean stolen credit cards. Now attackers steal compute and free-trial tokens. Free-trial abuse at some large customers has grown 4x in six months, because in AI, every prompt has real cost. Stripe is blocking 250,000 fraudulent free trials per week for one large customer. AI companies are growing faster than any cohort Stripe has ever tracked — top companies hitting $30M ARR in 18 months, roughly 3x faster than the 2018 SaaS class. And so far, this appears to be net new spend rather than cannibalized software budgets.

Emily Glassberg Sands also described Stripe's new Link product: a consumer wallet specifically designed for delegated agent purchases, allowing people to set spending controls on what their agents can buy on their behalf.
