### Aaron Levie | Box CEO
**@levie**

"If AI can make employees more productive, which is widely accepted as fact, then companies are going to want as many productive units of labor as possible. This is a key reason why I am changing my mind."

This explains Jevons paradox with AI. The better AI gets at performing tasks, the more companies will hire surrounding support staff. A small business that couldn't afford engineering can now hire engineers with 5-10X impact. The sales team automating outreach will hire more sales people because they have more leads. Counterintuitively, AI improvement leads to more hiring.
