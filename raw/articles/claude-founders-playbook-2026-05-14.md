## 原文内容

AI is reshaping how startups are built. Founders who have never written a line of code are shipping production-grade applications, hitting revenue before scaling headcount, and building tools to automate the most tedious workflows. The founder's role is shifting from individual contributor to **orchestrator**, enabling them to focus on the work only they can do.

### The Playbook

A practical playbook for building an AI-native startup, remapping the four core stages of a startup lifecycle — **Idea, MVP, Launch, and Scale** — into what's possible in 2026. Each stage includes goals, exit criteria, common failure modes, and AI-powered exercises.

### Six Core Themes

1. **Problem Validation & Market Research**
   How to use AI to validate problem hypotheses, map the competitive landscape, and conduct customer discovery

2. **MVP Architecture & Security Practices**
   Architecture, scope, and security practices to prevent AI-generated MVP codebases from accumulating technical debt

3. **Product-Market Fit Measurement Framework**
   Measurement frameworks that distinguish genuine product-market fit from early hype

4. **Launch-Stage Operating System**
   A launch-stage operating system that replaces founder attention with agentic workflows

5. **Product Matrix**
   A product matrix for when and how to use **Chat, Claude Cowork, and Claude Code** at each startup stage

6. **Founder Stories**
   Stories from founders at **Ambral, Anything, Carta Healthcare, HumanLayer, Vulcan Technologies**

### Target Audience

Best practices written for founders who are deciding from day one how to build a company around AI, and the early operators helping them do it.
