# ProFound

## Overview

**ProFound** is an AI-era marketing platform that helps companies understand and improve their visibility in AI search results (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.). Founded by **James Cadwallader**. Serves approximately **12% of the Fortune 500**.

## What It Does

1. **AI Search Visibility Monitoring**: Track how your brand appears across different AI platforms' responses
2. **Sentiment & Theme Extraction**: Understand what AI says alongside your brand mentions
3. **Source & Citation Analysis**: Reveal which web sources different models use to answer category questions
4. **Agent Deployment**: Build and deploy custom marketing agents that process data (e.g., sales call objection capture → battle card generation)

## Market Position

- Category: **AEO / ALG (Answer Engine Optimization / Agent-Led Growth)**
- Competitors: Emerging space, few direct competitors at enterprise scale
- Differentiation: Cross-platform monitoring (all major AI platforms) + agent deployment layer
- Business Model: SaaS for enterprises
