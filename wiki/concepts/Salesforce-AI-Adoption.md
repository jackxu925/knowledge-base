# Salesforce AI Adoption

## Overview

Salesforce's deep dive into AI agent adoption with Claude Code serves as a landmark case study for enterprise AI transformation.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Migration Timeline | 231 days | 13 days |
| Endpoints per PR | - | 21 |
| Test Coverage | - | 100% |
| Incident Rate | Baseline | -5% |

## Key Insights

1. **Not Just Faster, But Different** — The teams seeing the biggest wins from AI are completely changing how they work, not speeding up what they already do.

2. **Quality and Productivity** — Productivity vs. quality is sometimes framed as a tradeoff. Salesforce's case shows they are not mutually exclusive.

3. **Security Integration** — They built security guardrails and quality standards into the agentic workflow itself.

4. **End-to-End Ownership** — The key question: "What steps can you delete, what handoffs go away, what can an agent just own end to end?"

## Implications

- Enterprise AI adoption is not just about efficiency
- Quality can actually improve alongside increased output
- Security guardrails must be built into the agentic workflow
- Organizations need to rethink their entire processes, not just automate existing ones
