# Agent-Led Growth (ALG)

## Definition

**Agent-Led Growth (ALG)** is a marketing paradigm where the primary audience for content and brand visibility is not human consumers but **AI agents** that browse the web on behalf of users. It represents the successor to SEO (Search Engine Optimization) in an era where ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI systems replace blue-link search as the primary discovery mechanism.

## Core Principles

### Content Built for Agents, Not Humans

In traditional SEO, content was optimized for algorithms but consumed by humans. In ALG:

- **Content may never be seen by a human**: You build pages specifically for agent consumption
- **Agents use 100x more surface area**: A showerhead query used 65 web pages vs. human's 3-5 clicks
- **Long tail matters more**: Agents don't have cognitive bandwidth limits; they exhaustively research
- **Original insight is the only moat**: Agents have access to everything on the internet. To influence them, you need information that doesn't exist online yet ("the fleshy API" problem)

### Platform-Specific Strategies

| Platform | Primary Data Source | Key Lever |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini/Google** | YouTube heavily | Video content strategy |
| **ChatGPT/OpenAI** | Reddit (consumer), LinkedIn (B2B) | Community presence |
| **Claude/Anthropic** | Shifting toward real-time web | Fresh, structured data |

### The Dead Internet Timeline

James Cadwallader predicts **dead Internet (agent-only browsing) within 3 years**. The causal chain:

1. Humans stop visiting websites (agents answer all queries)
2. Ad revenue collapses (no human eyes on pages)
3. Publisher business models break
4. Editorial content incentives disappear
5. **Vertical integration becomes necessary**: AI labs acquire social networks to access fresh human data

## Advertising Evolution in ALG

Future advertising won't be display banners or sponsored links:

- **Ads = System Prompts**: Brands provide knowledge bases + targeting instructions
- **Hyper-personalization**: AI inserts brand mentions at the exact conversational moment of maximum responsiveness
- **The Objectivity Paradox**: For B2B agents, we demand our shopping agents be unbiased even though humans are easily swayed by branding

## Relationship to Existing Concepts

- **[[SEO]]**: ALG is not SEO. Different audience (agent vs. human), different surface area (100x), different success metrics.
- **[[AEO]] (Answer Engine Optimization)**: AEO is about appearing in AI answers; ALG is broader, encompassing full marketing operations via agents.
- **[[Digital-Marketing]]**: Traditional digital marketing assumes human audiences. ALG assumes superintelligent agent audiences with infinite bandwidth.

## Implications for Builders

1. **Visibility tools become essential**: You can't optimize what you can't measure across ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini
2. **API/MCP availability is a competitive moat**: Products without APIs will be invisible to agents
3. **Marketing teams must use agents to do marketing**: Using AI for marketing has shifted from nice-to-have to must-have
4. **Tool pages and structured data**: Every product feature needs its own indexable page for agent discovery
